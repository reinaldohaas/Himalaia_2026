## 1. Introduction and Methodological Scope

This register systematically compiles, cross-references, and evaluates the **peer-reviewed scientific literature** published on the 15 major high-mountain mass-flow catastrophes across the Trans-Himalayan orogen from 1980 to 2026.

For every event, this register records:
1. **Primary Scientific Citations:** Leading papers published in high-impact journals (*Science*, *Nature Geoscience*, *Geophysical Research Letters*, *Current Science*, *Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences*, *Journal of Glaciology*, *Mountain Research and Development*).
2. **Satellite & Remote Sensing Platforms Utilized:** Specific optical, radar (SAR), and thermal sensors deployed by researchers to reconstruct pre- and post-disaster topography.
3. **In-situ Instrument Telemetry:** Seismic stations, acoustic/infrasound sensors, weather stations (AWS), and river stream gauges.
4. **Physical Parameters Reported:** Peak flood discharge ($Q_{\text{peak}}$), total mobilized volume ($V_{\text{total}}$), liquid water volume ($V_{\text{water}}$), seismic magnitude ($M$), and measured precipitation.
5. **Key Empirical Anomalies & Strategic Value for Our Paper:** Critical physical discrepancies highlighted in each paper and how our research leverages these findings to validate the **Toró / Solar Electrodynamic ($J_z$)** paradigm while preempting conventional peer-review critiques.
