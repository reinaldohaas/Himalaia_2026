### Dear INTERMAGNET Executive Committee and Data Curators,

I am writing to formally request vector geomagnetic observatory records from the INTERMAGNET network for an ongoing physical investigation conducted at the Department of Physics of the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC, Brazil).

Our project investigates the electrodynamic and space weather conditions preceding and coinciding with extreme weather events and high-mountain slope failures in the Central Himalayas during late August 2026. Specifically, a major solar flare (GOES M6.9 flare in Active Region 4513, peaking at 10:02 UTC on 25 August 2026) occurred approximately 17 hours prior to the catastrophic Lhende Khola rock-ice collapse on 26 August 2026 (02:52:10 UTC).

#### 1. Scientific Objectives
To replace simplified theoretical parametric models with empirical measurements, our objective is to:
1. Extract calibrated baseline-corrected geomagnetic vector variations ($\Delta X, \Delta Y, \Delta Z, \Delta F$) and calculate real local $dH/dt$ and $dD/dt$ time derivatives across the Asian sector.
2. Evaluate actual ionospheric current system perturbations (solar quiet $S_q$, equatorial electrojet, and auroral electrojet extension) during the 20–28 August 2026 window.
3. Compute the exact regional geomagnetic disturbance field ($D_{\text{st}}$ and local $K$-indices) to quantify whether magnetospheric-ionospheric coupling played any observable role in local atmospheric electricity variations.

#### 2. Specific Data Requested
We respectfully request definitive, quasi-definitive, or calibrated variation data (in standard **IAGA-2002** ASCII or CDF format) at **1-second** and **1-minute** sampling intervals:

* **Target Observatories:**
  1. **Lhasa, Tibet, China (`LSA`)** — Lat: 29.64°N, Lon: 91.03°E
  2. **Beijing / Ming Tombs, China (`BJI`)** — Lat: 40.30°N, Lon: 116.20°E
  3. **Urumqi, Xinjiang, China (`WMQ`)** — Lat: 43.82°N, Lon: 87.70°E
  4. **Alibag, India (`ABG`)** — Lat: 18.64°N, Lon: 72.87°E
  5. **Sabhawala, India (`SAB`)** — Lat: 30.34°N, Lon: 77.80°E
  6. **Phu Thuy, Vietnam (`PHT`)** — Lat: 21.03°N, Lon: 105.95°E.

* **Time Interval:**
  - **Start Date:** 2026-08-20 00:00:00 UTC
  - **End Date:** 2026-08-28 23:59:59 UTC (9-day continuous span).

#### 3. Ethical and Attribution Undertaking
* Data will be used **strictly for non-commercial, academic research**.
* INTERMAGNET and the national operating institutes (Institute of Geophysics of the China Earthquake Administration, Indian Institute of Geomagnetism, etc.) will be fully credited and cited in all publications following the official INTERMAGNET data citation policy with appropriate DOIs.
* Processing workflows and data analysis scripts will be deposited openly in the study's reproducible GitHub repository.
