### Respected Directors and Colleagues,

I am writing to respectfully establish academic contact and request scientific data regarding the catastrophic high-mountain mass movement event that originated on **26 August 2026** in the Lhende Khola upper catchment, located in Gyirong County (Tibet Autonomous Region, China) near the border with Nepal.

The event produced two substantial seismic landslide signals cataloged by international networks:
- **USGS NEIC `us7000tbwb`:** Origin time 02:52:10 UTC, Magnitude Ms 5.2 (surface-wave inversion, depth 0.0 km, classified as `landslide`).
- **USGS NEIC `us7000tc90`:** Origin time 06:00:35 UTC, Magnitude Ms 4.2 (depth 0.0 km, classified as `landslide`).

#### 1. Scientific Objectives
Our research group at the Department of Physics of the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC, Brazil) is conducting a detailed physical investigation into the mechanics of this high-altitude slope failure and glacial lake breach:
1. Reconstructing the **seismic source time function (STF)** through force-inversion modeling to quantify the sliding mass acceleration, trajectory, basal friction coefficient, and total displaced volume ($V_{\text{bulk}}$).
2. Constraining the boundary conditions of thermal and pluvial forcing prior to slope failure using in-situ weather station records on the Tibetan side of the Himalayan crest.
3. Providing an unbiased, reproducible physical assessment of high-mountain geohazards under shifting climatic regimes on the Qinghai-Tibetan Plateau.

#### 2. Specific Data Requested
We respectfully request access to the following scientific observations for the period **25 August 2026 00:00 UTC to 27 August 2026 00:00 UTC**:

* **A. Regional Broadband Seismic Waveforms:**
  - Continuous 3-component broadband recordings (channels `BHZ`, `BHN`, `BHE`, sample rate $\ge 50\,\text{Hz}$) from regional permanent or portable seismic stations in southern Tibet, particularly:
    - Gyirong (Jilong) station
    - Tingri station
    - Nyalam station
    - Shigatse (Xigaze) network stations.

* **B. High-Altitude Meteorological Telemetry:**
  - Automatic Weather Station (AWS) records in Gyirong County (e.g., Gyirong Valley Observation Station / CAS Field Station):
    - Air temperature at 2 m (°C)
    - Precipitation rate and accumulation (mm/h)
    - Relative humidity, atmospheric pressure, and incoming solar radiation ($W/m^2$).

* **C. Pre- and Post-Event Remote Sensing / Drone Observations (if accessible):**
  - High-resolution orthoimagery or digital elevation models (DEM) of the Lhende Khola source zone.

#### 3. Institutional Commitment and Collaboration
* All data provided will be used **solely for academic, non-commercial research purposes**.
* Full institutional credit, citation, and acknowledgments will be given to ITPR, CAS, and CENC in all publications.
* We actively invite scientists from ITPR/CAS to join as co-authors in any peer-reviewed scientific paper resulting from this collaborative data analysis.
