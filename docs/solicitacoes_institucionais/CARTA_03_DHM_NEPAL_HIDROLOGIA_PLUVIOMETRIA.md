### Respected Director General,

I have the honor to address you on behalf of the Department of Physics at the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC), Brazil, to convey our deepest sympathies for the severe loss of life and infrastructure caused by the catastrophic debris flow and flood event that struck the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli river corridors on **26 August 2026**.

Our research group is conducting a comprehensive fluid-mechanical and hydraulic investigation aimed at reconstructing the hydrograph, peak discharge, flow velocity, and water-sediment volume of this unprecedented event to assist scientific understanding and hazard mitigation in high-mountain transboundary catchments.

#### 1. Purpose of Investigation
Hydraulic routing calculations indicate that the flood surge reached extreme peak discharges ($Q_{\text{peak}} \sim 1,500 - 3,500\,\text{m}^3/\text{s}$) along the upper Trishuli gorge, causing severe damage to road bridges, hydropower plants, and riverside communities.

To calibrate numerical flood routing models (Saint-Venant 1D/2D shallow water equations) and constrain rainfall-runoff budgets, empirical in-situ hydrometric and meteorological records from DHM monitoring stations are essential.

#### 2. Requested Hydrometeorological Data
We respectfully request the official recorded time-series (at 10-minute, 15-minute, or hourly resolution, as available) for the period **24 August 2026 to 28 August 2026**:

* **A. Hydrometric / River Gauging Stations (Bhote Koshi / Trishuli River):**
  1. **Timure (Station ID: 447)** — River stage (m) and discharge ($m^3/s$)
  2. **Syabrubesi (Station ID: 445)** — River stage (m) and discharge ($m^3/s$)
  3. **Betrawati (Station ID: 448)** — River stage (m) and discharge ($m^3/s$)
  4. **Trishuli Bazaar / Mailung / Kalikasthan** hydrometric telemetry.

* **B. Meteorological / Rainfall Stations (Rasuwa and Nuwakot Districts):**
  1. **Dhunche** (Station ID: 1055) — Precipitation accumulation (mm) and temperature
  2. **Timure AWS** — High-cadence rainfall records
  3. **Langtang Kyangjin AWS** — Rainfall and temperature
  4. **Nuwakot / Bidur** — Precipitation records.

#### 3. Academic and Non-Commercial Undertaking
* All requested data will be utilized **strictly for non-commercial, academic research**.
* Any data fees or administrative costs required by DHM regulations will be promptly settled by our institution.
* The Department of Hydrology and Meteorology of Nepal (DHM) will be formally acknowledged and cited as the primary data provider in all resulting scientific articles, conference presentations, and technical documentation.
* We would welcome scientific co-authorship or technical collaboration with DHM researchers interested in this event reconstruction.

Thank you very much for your kind consideration and assistance in advancing science and mountain hazard safety.
