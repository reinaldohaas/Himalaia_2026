### Dear Director and vDEC Evaluation Committee,

I am writing to formally submit an academic research request for International Monitoring System (IMS) calibrated infrasound data through the CTBTO Virtual Data Exploitation Centre (vDEC).

Our research group at the Department of Physics of the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC, Brazil) is currently conducting an independent scientific investigation into the catastrophic mass movement, rock-ice avalanche, and subsequent debris flow that occurred on **26 August 2026** in the Lhende Khola / Bhote Koshi / Trishuli River valley along the China–Nepal border (epicentral coordinates approximately 28.30°N, 85.35°E).

This extreme event triggered major seismic signals cataloged by the USGS NEIC (Event ID `us7000tbwb`, Ms 5.2 `landslide` at 02:52:10 UTC; Event ID `us7000tc90`, Ms 4.2 `landslide` at 06:00:35 UTC) and resulted in devastating downstream impacts, including the destruction of numerous bridges and severe human casualties in Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts.

#### 1. Scientific Objective
The objective of this research is to:
1. Detect and isolate the low-frequency acoustic and infrasonic signatures (0.01 – 10 Hz) generated by the high-altitude mass detachment, supersonic air displacement (air-blast), and dynamic canyon propagation.
2. Estimate the acoustic energy release and compare it with the kinetic and seismic energy estimations derived from USGS seismic inversion.
3. Determine the precise multi-stage timing of the catastrophic failure (discrimination between the initial cliff collapse, moraine breach, and downstream debris propagation).

#### 2. Specific Data Requested
We respectfully request access to continuous raw and calibrated microbarometer waveform telemetry (channels `BDF`, sample rate 20 Hz):
* **Target Stations:**
  - **IS31** (Aktyubinsk, Kazakhstan)
  - **IS52** (Diego Garcia)
  - **IS44** (Petropavlovsk-Kamchatskiy, Russia / regional Central Asian arrays)
  - Any auxiliary or regional IMS infrasound array within a 3,500 km range of the epicenter (28.30°N, 85.35°E).
* **Time Interval:**
  - **Start:** 2026-08-25 18:00:00 UTC
  - **End:** 2026-08-26 18:00:00 UTC (24-hour continuous window).

#### 3. Compliance and Ethical Commitment
In full compliance with CTBTO vDEC terms of reference:
* The data will be used **strictly and exclusively for non-commercial, academic scientific research**.
* Data will be held securely in accordance with CTBTO data policies and will not be redistributed to third parties without prior authorization.
* CTBTO IDC and the IMS network will be formally cited and acknowledged in all resulting peer-reviewed publications and technical reports.
* Full reproducibility protocols, including scripts and processing methodology, will be deposited in public academic repositories upon paper publication.

We thank you in advance for your consideration of this request and look forward to your positive response.
