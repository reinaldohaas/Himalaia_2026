### Dear Prof. Holzworth and WWLLN Consortium Colleagues,

I am writing to formally request stroke-level lightning telemetry from the World Wide Lightning Location Network (WWLLN) for a focused academic investigation conducted at the Department of Physics of the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC, Brazil).

Our project investigates the severe hydrometeorological and geophysical dynamics surrounding the catastrophic multi-stage rock-ice avalanche and flash debris flow that devastated the Lhende Khola and Bhote Koshi / Trishuli river valleys on **26 August 2026** (approximate coordinates 28.30°N, 85.35°E).

#### 1. Scientific Context and Purpose
Eyewitness accounts from downstream settlements (Timure, Rasuwagadhi, and Syabrubesi) and high-altitude pastoralists reported prolonged nocturnal rumbling sounds, intense flash luminescence, and sudden meteorological destabilization preceding the devastating flow surges.

Our objective is to perform an rigorous spatiotemporal analysis of convective electrical activity in the complex topography of the Langtang Himal:
1. Determine the exact distribution of lightning strokes (microsecond timing, geographical coordinates, radiated VLF energy in Joules, and estimated peak current in kA).
2. Assess whether localized convective cells with anomalous charge separation were active in the upper glacial cirque (4,500 – 7,200 m a.s.l.) during the 24 hours preceding the primary seismic event (02:52:10 UTC on 26/08/2026).
3. Test physical hypotheses concerning convective rainfall intensity, thunderstorm-induced cloudburst mechanisms, and orographic charge enhancement.

#### 2. Specific Data Requirements
* **Geographical Bounding Box:**
  - Latitude: **27.00°N to 29.50°N**
  - Longitude: **84.00°E to 87.00°E**
  - Centered on the upper Trishuli / Gyirong basin (approx. 100 km radius around 28.30°N, 85.35°E).
* **Time Interval:**
  - **Start:** 2026-08-25 00:00:00 UTC
  - **End:** 2026-08-26 23:59:59 UTC (48-hour continuous window).
* **Desired Data Fields:**
  - Date and time (UTC, microsecond resolution)
  - Latitude and Longitude (WGS84)
  - Stroke radiated VLF energy (Joules) and uncertainty
  - Estimated peak current (kA)
  - Station count (number of receiving stations participating in the TOGA solution)
  - Timing residual / chi-square fit quality metric.

#### 3. Terms of Use and Ethical Commitment
* The data will be used **exclusively for non-commercial, academic scientific research**.
* If required by the University of Washington, we are fully prepared to execute a standard Academic Data Use Agreement (DUA) or collaborate with your team.
* The WWLLN network and University of Washington will be explicitly cited and acknowledged in all resulting peer-reviewed publications and public academic repositories.
